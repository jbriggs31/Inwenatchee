Events & Happenings

Recurring favorites and seasonal highlights around the valley.

Wenatchee Valley Farmers Market

Saturdays, 8 AM – 1 PM · May to late October · Pybus Public Market, 3 N Worthen St, Wenatchee

North Central Washington's largest farmers market, in the Pybus Public Market parking lot, with local fruit and vegetables, breads, cheeses, honey, crafts and live music. The 2026 season runs through October 31; a winter market is also held in November and December.

wenatcheefarmersmarket.com

Pybus Friday Night Music

Friday evenings · Pybus Public Market

Local musicians play Friday evenings at Pybus Public Market on the riverfront. Performers change each week; see the Pybus events page for who is playing.

Pybus events & news

Washington State Apple Blossom Festival

Each spring (late April – early May) · Memorial Park and downtown Wenatchee

Washington's oldest festival, held during apple blossom season. Expect parades (including the Stemilt Grand Parade and Classy Chassis car parade), a carnival, a food fair, live music and the annual Apple Blossom musical at the Numerica PAC.

Festival calendar

Wenatchee Wild Hockey

Fall – winter season · Town Toyota Center, 1300 Walla Walla Ave, Wenatchee

The Wenatchee Wild play WHL hockey at Town Toyota Center. Buy tickets online, in person or by phone through the arena.

Wild schedule & tickets

Numerica Performing Arts Center

123 N Wenatchee Ave, Wenatchee

An intimate 550-seat downtown theater with Broadway and national touring shows, local productions and community events.
