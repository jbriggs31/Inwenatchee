Food & Drink

Each spot below was checked against its official website in October 2026.

Restaurants

McGlinn's Public House

111 Orondo Ave, Wenatchee

Wood-fired pizza, hand-pressed burgers and Northwest beer in the historic Garland Building. Open daily for lunch and dinner, with breakfast on weekends.

mcglinns.com

Saddle Rock Pub & Brewery

25 N Wenatchee Ave, Suite 107, Wenatchee

A downtown brewpub next to the Performing Arts Center, with craft beer and cider, local wine, and hand-tossed pizza.

saddlerockbrewery.com

The Wild Huckleberry & Huck at Pybus

302 S Mission St · Huck at Pybus: 3 N Worthen St, Wenatchee

A local favorite for breakfast and lunch, plus a full-day spot inside Pybus Public Market. They don't take reservations.

wildhuck.com

Wineries

Chateau Faire Le Pont

1 Vineyard Way, Wenatchee · Tasting room: 636 Front St, Leavenworth

A winery and restaurant in a renovated 1920s brick building, with an outdoor deck and Cascade views.

fairelepont.com

Crayelle Cellars

15 N 2nd St, Suite 103, Wenatchee

A cozy downtown tasting room that's handy before a show. Check the website for current tasting hours.

crayellecellars.com

Cideries

Union Hill Cider Co.

988 S Union Ave, East Wenatchee

A family-run cidery using fruit from its own orchards. The family- and pet-friendly taproom has 20 taps.

unionhillciderco.com

Snowdrift Cider Co.

273 S Ward Ave, East Wenatchee

An internationally recognized, family-owned craft cidery and orchard with a tasting room.

snowdriftcider.com
