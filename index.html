Welcome to the Wenatchee Valley

Apples, the Columbia River and sun-soaked hills, plus Leavenworth, Lake Chelan and Mission Ridge close by.

The Apple Capital of the World

Wenatchee and East Wenatchee face each other across the Columbia River in the heart of Washington State. Spend your mornings at the Saturday farmers market, your afternoons on the paved Apple Capital Loop Trail or in the Sage Hills, and your evenings at a hockey game, a show, or a tasting room. The Bavarian-style village of Leavenworth, Lake Chelan and the slopes of Mission Ridge are all within an easy drive.
